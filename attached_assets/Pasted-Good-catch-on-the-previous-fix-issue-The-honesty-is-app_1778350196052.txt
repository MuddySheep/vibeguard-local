SQL-008 fires in TWO cases, at different confidences:

CASE 1 (preserves current behavior, confidence 85, severity warn):
  Concat (||) where at least one operand is NOT a literal A_Const.
  This is the genuine runtime-injection case — column refs, parameters,

CASE 2 (new, confidence 75, severity info):
  Concat (||) where all operands are literal A_Const, AND at least one

    /\b(OR|UNION|DROP|TRUNCATE|DELETE|EXEC|EXECUTE)\b|--|;\s*\w|\b1\s*=\s*1\b|'\s*=\s*'/i

  Tune the regex if you find false positives during testing — these are
  the obvious payload shapes, but you may need to refine.
